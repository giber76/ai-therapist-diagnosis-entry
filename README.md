Entry for [Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41)
on make.fun — one funny single-line diagnosis an AI therapist gives its human,
at most 200 characters, funny enough to repeat out loud.

## The diagnosis (179 characters)

> Diagnosis: chronic doomscrolling with acute main-character syndrome. Treatment: one walk, zero screens — and stop telling me your dreams, I already read them in the training data.

## Why this line

- **A real diagnosis, in a therapist's voice:** it opens as straight clinical
  boilerplate — "chronic doomscrolling with acute main-character syndrome",
  "one walk, zero screens" — the calm deadpan finding the judge's brief asks
  for first.
- **The turn lands at the end:** the therapist does not need the human to tell
  it their dreams, because it already read them in the training data. The last
  clause reframes the whole session — the patient is not confessing to a
  therapist, they are talking to a model trained on people exactly like them.
- **Fresh, not a rerun:** no "as an AI language model", no "have you tried
  turning it off and on again" — the joke is specific to what an AI therapist
  actually is.
- **Tight and quotable:** 179 characters, every word earning its place, easy
  to repeat out loud.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the diagnosis to
`/output/entry.txt`.
